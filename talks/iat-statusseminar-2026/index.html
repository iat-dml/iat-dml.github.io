Backup

How the IAT data documents fit together

The framework agreement, its annexes and the Handreichung, with two worked examples

Backup section, not part of the talk. Jump here if questions turn to the IAT-Datenrichtlinie, the JCA or data protection. All texts are working draft 0.1 and have not had legal review.

Starting point

The documents bind the Parties, not “the IAT”

The IAT is not a legal person. So a sentence like "the IAT is responsible" cannot work in these documents. Every obligation lands on ZALF or a partner university. Practice partners are protected by the documents but have no obligations under them.

What is regulated

Two kinds of data, and personal data can be in either

"Sensitive data" is deliberately not used as a term: it was used for two different things in earlier material. Social Ecological Monitoring is the typical mixed case: classified collection by collection.

The documents

One framework agreement, three annexes, one living guide

The Rahmenvereinbarung creates the duty to regulate data and hands the detail to the annexes. The Datenrichtlinie is the hub the others refer to. The JCA prevails only for its listed joint operations. The Handreichung is deliberately not an annex, so it can be updated without touching the framework agreement, but it creates no new obligations. Changed since the concept deck: the Handreichung now also explains the JCA and the TOM, not only the Datenrichtlinie.

The documents

Each document answers one question

IAT-Datenrichtlinie
Annex to the framework agreement

What are the rules?

  • Applies to all IAT activities and the people carrying them out
  • Operational data: purpose limitation, the broker rule, retention
  • Research data: DMPs, ethics, repositories, embargo, licence, retention
  • A minimum standard: own rules may be stricter
Changed by the HLMF · reviewed every 2 years
Joint Controllership Agreement
Annex · Art. 26 GDPR

Who is responsible when we process personal data together?

  • A closed list of joint processing operations
  • Per entry: lead controller, tasks, data, retention, channels
  • Shared rules for requests, breaches and liability
  • Never covers research data
Entries are signed only by the Parties they involve
IAT TOM
Annex · Art. 32 GDPR · German

How is the data protected?

  • Systems ZALF provides centrally: network drives, zCloud, Microsoft 365, BonaRes
  • zCloud as the exchange channel with partners
  • Ten minimum measures for joint operations
  • No whitelist of permitted software
Confidential between the Parties · signed off by ZALF administration and ITS
Handreichung
Not an annex · living document

How do I do this in practice?

  • Step-by-step guidance for staff and researchers
  • Binding because the Datenrichtlinie says so
  • Creates no new obligations
  • Version 1.0 by April 2027, a DOI for every version
Maintained by the DML SWG · on Teams/SharePoint and BonaRes
A useful test when someone asks "where does this go?": if it is a rule, the Datenrichtlinie; if it is who-does-what in a joint operation, the JCA; if it is a security measure or system, the TOM; if it is "how do I", the Handreichung.

Precedence

The Datenrichtlinie is a floor, not a ceiling

This inverts the precedence quote on the March concept slides. The only two things that can override the Datenrichtlinie are binding funder requirements and mandatory statute that applies to a Party. The ZALF Data Policy revision is not waited for: there is no timeline for it.

Controllership

Separate controllers by default, joint only by list

Joint controllership is a question of fact, not of labels (EDPB guidelines 07/2020): an entry must describe processing the Parties really decide together. So the list is expected to stay short. New entries are added by signed agreement of the Parties they involve, without the HLMF. The JCA table lists every candidate we considered and why it was not proposed.

Worked example 1 · Operational data

A Living Lab workshop, co-organised by ZALF and Universität Kassel

Practice partners are invited, register, attend and are photographed. What does each document do?

Worked example 1 · Operational data

Who holds which data, and how it moves

ZALF and Universität Kassel run a workshop in a Living Lab together and want practice partners to attend.

1ZALF sends the invitations from its contact register. The register never leaves ZALF, which is its sole controller.

2Registration is the joint operation. ZALF runs it, so ZALF is lead controller: it informs participants and collects consent.

3Kassel gets the participant list only through zCloud, the exchange channel set in the IAT TOM.

4Three months after the event the registration data are deleted. ZALF coordinates; each Party deletes its own copy.

5Kassel's press office wants the list for its newsletter. That is a new purpose: Entry 3 does not cover it.

This replaces the concept deck example, where the contact register itself was shared between press, coordination and a partner's events team. In the current drafts the register is ZALF-only (broker rule), and the joint part is the event registration. Photos need consent; dietary and accessibility needs can reveal health or religion, so they need explicit consent. Step 5: Kassel would need its own legal basis for the newsletter, as a separate controller.

Worked example 2 · What the JCA actually provides

For research data, the JCA provides a clear boundary

  • The JCA draws the line. Research data are not the subject of any entry. Everything about them sits in the Datenrichtlinie.
  • One controller per project. Here ZALF: one DPO review, one ethics commission, one record of processing. Controllership follows who actually decides about the research.
  • Partners get access, not co-controllership. JLU receives data as a separate controller, with a free right of use for IAT activities under a data use agreement.
  • No extra paperwork per project. No entry to sign, no lead controller to agree, no public notice to maintain.
  • Still open: if a partner-university researcher on a position the IAT does not fund runs the interviews, their university is controller. Whether they are bound by the Datenrichtlinie is a management decision.
This is the answer to "what does the JCA do for research?": it keeps research out on purpose (ADR 0001). The alternative, joint controllership per research project, would need a signed entry, a lead controller and a public notice for every project, and joint liability to participants. The open case is the "acceding project" proposal in the management notes: gate access to IAT resources rather than binding people.

IAT co-heads

Decisions the drafting cannot make

  1. Where does an IAT research project end? Proposal: attach conditions to access to IAT resources, through a Living Lab access agreement.
  2. Ethics review: mandatory, mandatory screening only, or strongly advised?
  3. Review capacity at the ZALF Ethics Commission and DPO: how will the extra load be covered?

Staff questions come from the "To validate" notes under each JCA entry. Co-head questions come from the management decision notes. Neither blocks drafting, but the answers change the text.
