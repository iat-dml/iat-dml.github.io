We ran out of chairs.

More than 40 people came to our Quarto workshop at the ZALF Science Conference 2026 on 19 November, so we fetched extra chairs from the neighbouring rooms to fit everyone in.

Quarto is an open-source publishing system that turns plain-text files with code into manuscripts, slides, websites and dashboards. We first gave the workshop at iEMSs 2026 in Dublin in July. For ZALF we extended it to 90 minutes and added a guided demo: participants built a small manuscript on their own laptops, step by step, with numbers in the text taken straight from the data, a cross-referenced figure and citations. One render then produced a PDF, a web page and a Word document from the same source.

To pitch the workshop at the start of the conference, we made a short animation, "A year in research". It follows one researcher through the problems most of us know: the reasons behind decisions get lost, numbers in the manuscript fall behind the latest results, and figures copied into slides go out of date.

Thanks to Isabel Nicholson-Thomas and Manuel Kurmann (ETH Zurich), who developed the material with us, and to the conference organisers.
